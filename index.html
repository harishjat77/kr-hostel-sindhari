About the hostel

Residential care built around a student’s daily progress.

K. R. Aadarsh Hostel & Coaching Center combines accommodation, school support and guided study for students from Classes 1 to 12. The residential routine is designed around learning, meals, rest, recreation and communication with families.

Students receive support for Hindi- and English-medium schooling, daily coaching and selected school entrance preparation while living at the Sindhari campus.
