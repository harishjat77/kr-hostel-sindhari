A hostel built around student life.

Accommodation, learning support and a thoughtful daily routine come together at the K. R. campus in Sindhari.

Who we are

More than a place to stay.

K. R. Aadarsh Hostel & Coaching Center supports school-going students from Classes 1 to 12 through residential care, school coordination and guided study.

The campus routine is designed around study, meals, rest, recreation and family communication. Students attending Hindi- and English-medium schools can receive support while living in one organized residential setting.

Our approach

Four parts of a balanced hostel routine.

Residential support works best when the student’s complete day is considered, not only classroom hours.

01

Study

Guided preparation, regular coaching and support aligned with school learning.

02

03

Educational activities, sports and parent interaction beyond daily lessons.
